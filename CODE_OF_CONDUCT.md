# Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

We want responsive-audit to be a useful, low-friction project for developers of every
background and experience level. Be respectful, assume good intent, and critique the work
rather than the person.

## Expected behavior

- Give actionable, technical feedback with evidence.
- Keep issues and pull requests focused on the project.
- Respect privacy: do not post confidential URLs, credentials, storage state, or private
  screenshots.
- Credit other people's work and ideas.

## Unacceptable behavior

- Harassment, discrimination, threats, or personal attacks.
- Publishing private information without permission.
- Spam, repeatedly reopening resolved disputes, or derailing technical threads.
- Using the issue tracker to promote unrelated products.

## Scope

This applies to repository issues, pull requests, discussions, reviews, and any project
space managed by the maintainers.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit, hide, lock, or remove contributions that violate this policy, and may
temporarily or permanently restrict participation. Report conduct problems privately to the
maintainer through the contact on the GitHub profile. Reports are reviewed in good faith and
kept confidential.

This policy is intentionally short. It follows the spirit of the Contributor Covenant.
